### Current Positions

- Professor, Department of Psychiatry, Korea University College of Medicine & Korea University Anam Hospital
- Professor, Department of Biomedical Informatics and Medical Education, Korea University College of Medicine
- Director, Smart Healthcare Research Center, Korea University Anam Hospital (2024–)

### Research Interests

- **Digital psychiatry** — smartphone & wearable–based digital phenotyping for depression, anxiety, insomnia, ADHD, and obstructive sleep apnea
- **Sleep & circadian rhythms** — chronobiology-informed assessment and intervention
- **Machine learning in mental health** — state prediction and digital biomarkers from multimodal data
- **Digital therapeutics (DTx)** — development and clinical validation
- **VR / metaverse-based interventions** — immersive approaches to psychotherapy and mental health care
- **Medical education** — interprofessional education and AI integration in clinical training
